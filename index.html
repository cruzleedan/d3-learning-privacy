d3-learning Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

d3-learning is a small learning app run by the owner of a farm cafe and training site in the Philippines. Trainees use it to take courses on their phones; the owner uses it to write and manage those courses. This policy explains what information the app keeps, who can see it, and what it does with it. The app is private: it has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of visitors.

What information the app keeps

  • Trainee accounts: name, username, and a PIN or password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form).
  • Learning records: which lessons a trainee finished, quiz answers and scores, and the dates they happened.
  • Staff accounts: name, email address and password hash for the owner and instructors.
  • Course content: text, photos, videos, audio and PDFs that the owner uploads.
  • Technical data: a sign-in cookie, and settings stored in the browser (such as saved courses for offline use). Server logs record errors, not what people read.

Who can see it

Only the owner and instructors who manage the app can see trainee accounts and learning records. Trainees can see their own progress. Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising.

AI writing assistant

The owner can use an AI assistant to help write course material. What the owner types or attaches to the assistant is sent to the AI provider the app is set up to use (Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude or OpenAI). Trainee accounts and learning records are not sent to the assistant. On free plans, some providers may use submitted content to improve their products; the owner is warned about this inside the app.

Backups and Google Drive

The app makes a nightly backup of its database and uploaded files so they can be restored after a failure. The owner can choose to store these backups in their own Google Drive. If so:

  • The app asks for only the drive.file permission, which lets it see and manage only the files the backup itself creates. It cannot read any other file in the Google Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the server before they are uploaded, so Google stores only scrambled data.
  • The Google access is used only to upload, check, list and delete these backup files. It is not used for any other purpose, is not shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • The owner can stop this at any time by removing the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions and deleting the backup folder in Google Drive.

How long information is kept

Accounts and learning records are kept while the trainee is enrolled and are deleted on request. Database backups are kept for about 14 days; deleted files may stay in Google Drive's trash for up to 30 days.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete your information, or ask that your account be removed. Trainees should ask the owner or instructor at the training site. These rights follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Security

The app is served over HTTPS, passwords and PINs are hashed, accounts lock after repeated wrong attempts, and backups are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but these measures are kept up to date.

Children

The app is meant for adult trainees and staff. It is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Questions can be raised by opening an issue at github.com/cruzleedan/d3-learning-privacy/issues.
